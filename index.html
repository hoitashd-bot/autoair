The problem

Your Tires. Always Ready.

Tires lose pressure gradually. Keeping them right means checking the gauge, finding a compressor, connecting a hose and doing it all again a few weeks later. Many drivers put it off. Yet keeping the recommended pressure helps support tire life, handling and energy efficiency.

AutoAir is a compact electric pumping system designed to integrate with an EV's tire-pressure system. It is designed to top up air on its own, so the tires stay ready.

The technology

How AutoAir is designed to work.

Scroll through the proposed system. Each step lights up the part of the wheel involved. AutoAir is a concept in development, not a commercially available or fully validated product.

CONCEPT DIAGRAM · NOT TO SCALESTEP 1 OF 5
STEP 01 · POWER

The vehicle supplies electrical power.

An EV already carries a large battery. AutoAir is designed to draw a small amount of that power through the wheel hub, so the system never needs its own charging.

STEP 02 · SENSE

A pressure sensor detects the drop.

A sensor at the valve reads the air pressure inside the tire. Normal driving, temperature changes and time all cause slow pressure loss, and the sensor notices it.

STEP 03 · DECIDE

A controller decides when to act.

The control system compares the reading with the target pressure set for the vehicle. If the tire is below target, it signals the pump to start.

STEP 04 · INFLATE

A compact pump adds air.

A small electric pump mounted inside the wheel pushes air into the tire through a one-way valve, raising the pressure gradually.

STEP 05 · STOP

It stops at the target.

When the sensor confirms the target pressure, the controller switches the pump off and goes back to monitoring. Nothing for the driver to do.

Efficiency

Better Pressure. Smarter Driving.

An underinflated tire flexes more as it rolls, which increases rolling resistance. Rolling resistance takes energy, and in an EV that energy comes from the battery. Keeping tires at their recommended pressure helps avoid that unnecessary loss.

Support EV efficiencyCorrect pressure helps the vehicle use its energy as intended.
Support tire lifeTires kept at the right pressure tend to wear more evenly.
Smarter maintenanceSmall, timely top-ups instead of waiting until pressure is noticeably low.

AutoAir has not yet been tested for efficiency or range gains, and we don't quote figures we can't support.
